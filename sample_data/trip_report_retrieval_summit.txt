Trip report - Applied Retrieval Summit 2026, Austin (18-19 February 2026)
Author: Priya Desai

Dr. Chen's talk on hybrid retrieval in Halcyon drew the largest audience of the afternoon. She showed that adding lexical candidates fixed most failures on queries containing part numbers and ticket identifiers.

The most useful conversation was with Alice Moreno from Kestrel Logistics, who presented lessons from the Ember routing pilot. Alice argued that driver trust mattered more than solver quality: routes that drivers did not believe were simply ignored.

A university group presented work on calibrating retrieval confidence. Their main point was that systems should abstain when the top passages disagree, instead of forcing an answer.

Takeaways for Quillon Labs: add an abstention mode to Halcyon search, and log which retriever produced each result so failures can be debugged.
