# RFC: Project Lodestar patient record matching

Author: Ravi Narayan (Tech Lead, Brightwater Health)
Reviewers: Wei Zhang, Dr. Imani Okoye
Status: Accepted

## Problem

Brightwater Health operates four hospitals and 31 clinics in the Boston area. The same patient often has several medical record numbers because registration happens at different sites. An internal audit estimated that 8.1 percent of patient records were duplicates, which causes repeated lab orders and fragmented medication histories.

## Approach

Project Lodestar links records with probabilistic record linkage in the Fellegi-Sunter tradition. Candidate pairs are generated by blocking on date of birth plus the first three letters of the surname, and each pair is scored using name, date of birth, address and phone comparisons.

Pairs scoring above 0.92 are merged automatically. Pairs between 0.75 and 0.92 go to a manual review queue staffed by health information management, and pairs below 0.75 are kept separate.

## Implementation

The matching service is written in Rust for predictable latency. Registration events stream from the EHR through Apache Kafka, and nightly batch re-scoring runs in DuckDB over Parquet snapshots. Address standardization is provided by the vendor Corvid Data.

## Success metrics

The target is to reduce the duplicate rate below 3 percent within twelve months while keeping the false merge rate under 0.1 percent, measured by a manual audit of 1,000 sampled merges per quarter.
