Ember pilot check-in call - 4 December 2025
Participants: Samuel Okafor, Alice Moreno, Rui Santos (Lisbon depot manager)

Sam opened the call with mid-pilot numbers: the on-time delivery rate at the Lisbon depot reached 91 percent in week eight, up from 87 percent before the pilot.

Rui said drivers still distrust routes through the old town, where narrow streets block the larger vans. The team agreed to add road restrictions for vehicles longer than 6 metres in the Alfama district.

Alice Moreno reported that 31 of 42 drivers now follow the suggested route on most days. The remaining drivers mostly cover routes with recurring loading-dock delays.

Next steps: Rui will collect loading-dock wait times for two weeks, and Sam will ask finance whether Saturday shifts can be added in January.
